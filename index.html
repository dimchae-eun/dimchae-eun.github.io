Hi, I'm Chaeeun Kim

A proactive, evolving, and self-driven researcher.

I am a research assistant in Applied Bioengineering at Seoul National University, working at the intersection of electrochemistry, materials, and clinical diagnostics. My work centers on building biosensors and bioelectronic devices that bring accurate laboratory measurements directly to the point of care.
