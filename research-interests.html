Research

Research Interests

Making clinically relevant biochemical information measurable: on the body, at the point of care, and within engineered tissue models.

Research ExperiencesResearch Interests

Wearable & Continuous Biochemical Monitoring

Disease progression and treatment response unfold continuously, yet most biochemical tests capture a single moment. I am interested in wearable and minimally invasive sensors that track biomarkers in sweat and interstitial fluid over days, with sensing interfaces that stay accurate without repeated recalibration.

Point-of-Care Diagnostics

A laboratory-grade result helps most when it arrives at the moment of decision. Building on the clinical evaluation of a disposable potassium strip, I am interested in sensitive, low-cost diagnostics that patients and clinicians can trust at the bedside, at home, and in resource-limited settings.

Sensor-Integrated Organ‑on‑Chip

Organ-on-chip and organoid models reproduce key features of human tissue, but are usually read only at endpoints. I am interested in integrating biochemical sensors into these models to follow metabolic changes and drug responses in real time.
