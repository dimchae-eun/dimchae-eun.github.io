Clinical Evaluation of a Disposable Test Strip for Potassium Level Measurement in Patients Treated with Maintenance Hemodialysis

Cheol Ho Park†, Yujoon Kim†, Chaeeun Kim†, Chaehyung Kim†, Sowon Kim, Kangwon Lee, Tae-Hyun Yoo (†Equal contribution)

Clinical Journal of the American Society of Nephrology

↗ View Paper (DOI)

Abstract

Potassium imbalance is a serious clinical concern for people receiving maintenance hemodialysis, yet conventional laboratory testing can delay actionable results. We clinically evaluated a disposable potentiometric test strip and handheld reader for potassium measurement using blood samples from patients treated with maintenance hemodialysis. The study assessed agreement with a laboratory reference method and the repeatability of strip-based measurements using capillary and venous samples. These findings support the clinical potential of disposable potassium test strips for rapid point-of-care assessment and more timely potassium management.

Clinical protocol: each participant underwent repeated strip measurements using both finger-prick capillary blood and venous blood.
